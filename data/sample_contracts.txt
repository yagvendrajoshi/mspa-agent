MASTER LNG SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT
Standard Form — DES (Delivered Ex-Ship) 2019 Edition
Based on BP Standard Form MSPA and industry standard terms

Between: [Buyer] and [Seller]
Date: [Execution Date]

CLAUSE 1 — DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Allowed Laytime" means the period of time agreed for loading or
unloading operations as specified in the relevant Confirmation Notice,
being 36 hours for loading and 24 hours for unloading.

1.2 "Bill of Lading Date" means the date of the bill of lading issued
in respect of each LNG Cargo at the Loading Port.

1.3 "Confirmation Notice" means a written notice issued by either Party
confirming the commercial terms of an individual LNG transaction
including cargo quantity, price, delivery window, and demurrage rate.

1.4 "Demurrage Rate" means the rate payable for excess time used beyond
Allowed Laytime, expressed in USD per 24-hour period as specified in
each Confirmation Notice.

1.5 "Notice of Readiness" or "NOR" means the written notice tendered
by the master of the LNG Ship upon arrival at the Loading Port or
Discharge Port indicating the vessel is ready to load or discharge.

1.6 "LNG Ship" means the vessel nominated by Buyer for the purpose
of loading and transporting LNG Cargo under each Transaction.

1.7 "Completion of Loading" means the moment when the LNG Ship's
manifold is disconnected from the Loading Terminal's loading arm
following completion of loading operations.

CLAUSE 7 — LOADING, UNLOADING AND LAYTIME

7.1 Notice of Readiness shall be tendered by the LNG Ship master upon
arrival at the Loading Port whether in berth or not, in port or not,
in free pratique or not, and whether customs cleared or not.

7.2 Laytime shall commence upon the earlier of:
(a) six (6) hours after a valid NOR has been tendered; or
(b) when the LNG Ship is all fast at the loading berth and
the Loading Terminal has confirmed readiness to load.

7.3 The Allowed Laytime for loading operations shall be thirty-six (36)
hours from commencement of Laytime unless otherwise specified in the
applicable Confirmation Notice.

7.4 Time shall not count against Laytime or Demurrage when delay is
caused solely by:
(a) breakdown of the Loading Terminal's equipment;
(b) industrial action affecting the Loading Terminal; or
(c) force majeure events as defined in Clause 14.

7.5 Despatch shall be payable by Seller at fifty percent (50%) of the
applicable Demurrage Rate for all time saved if loading is completed
before the expiry of the Allowed Laytime.

CLAUSE 7.18 — DEMURRAGE

7.18(a) If the total time used for loading exceeds the Allowed Laytime,
Demurrage shall be payable by Buyer to Seller at the Demurrage Rate
specified in the applicable Confirmation Notice, which shall not be
less than USD 25,000 per 24-hour period, prorated for any partial
24-hour period. Each begun hour shall be fully paid.

7.18(b) Claims for Demurrage under this Clause 7.18 shall only be
payable if received by the other Party within sixty (60) Days
following Completion of Loading of the relevant LNG Cargo.
Claims submitted after this period shall be deemed waived.

7.18(c) Demurrage claims shall be supported by the following documents:
(i) Notice of Readiness;
(ii) Statement of Facts signed by the Terminal operator;
(iii) Vessel's protest if applicable;
(iv) Bill of Lading.

CLAUSE 9 — PRICING AND INVOICING

9.1 The Contract Sales Price for each LNG Cargo shall be determined
by reference to the Japan Korea Marker (JKM) as published by
S&P Global Platts for the relevant delivery month, plus or minus
the agreed differential as specified in the applicable
Confirmation Notice.

9.2 Provisional invoices shall be issued within five (5) Business Days
of the Bill of Lading Date based on the nominated cargo quantity
and the estimated price. Final invoices shall be issued within
thirty (30) Days of the relevant price determination date.

9.3 Payment of all invoices shall be made in USD by wire transfer
to the bank account notified by the receiving Party, within
five (5) Business Days of receipt of a valid invoice.

CLAUSE 10 — LNG QUALITY

10.1 The LNG delivered under this Agreement shall conform to the
following specifications:
(a) Gross Calorific Value: not less than 1,000 BTU per standard
cubic foot and not more than 1,150 BTU per standard cubic foot;
(b) Methane content: not less than 85.0 mol%;
(c) Hydrogen Sulphide: not more than 0.25 grains per 100 scf;
(d) Temperature at delivery: not warmer than minus 157 degrees Celsius.

CLAUSE 14 — FORCE MAJEURE

14.1 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for failure to perform
any obligation under this Agreement to the extent that such failure
is caused by a Force Majeure Event. A Force Majeure Event means any
event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the affected
Party, including but not limited to:
(a) acts of God, flood, drought, earthquake, or other natural disaster;
(b) epidemic or pandemic declared by the World Health Organization;
(c) war, armed conflict, terrorist attack, or civil war;
(d) riot, civil commotion, or industrial dispute not involving
the affected Party's own employees;
(e) government action, sanctions, or embargo;
(f) nuclear, chemical, or biological contamination.

14.2 The Party claiming Force Majeure shall:
(a) notify the other Party in writing within forty-eight (48) hours
of the occurrence of the Force Majeure Event;
(b) provide reasonable evidence of the Force Majeure Event within
seven (7) Days of the initial notification;
(c) use all reasonable endeavours to overcome or mitigate the
effects of the Force Majeure Event.

14.3 If a Force Majeure Event continues for a period exceeding ninety
(90) consecutive Days, either Party may terminate the affected
Transaction by giving not less than thirty (30) Days written notice.

CLAUSE 21 — GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

21.1 This Agreement and any Transaction hereunder shall be governed
by and construed in accordance with English law.

21.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement
shall be finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of the
London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA).
